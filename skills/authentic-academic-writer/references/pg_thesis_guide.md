# Postgraduate Thesis Writing Guide (Agriculture, Science, and Technology - ASET)

This reference operationalizes postgraduate thesis requirements, drawing directly from university doctoral and master's degree regulations (specifically codified in the **JOSTUM PG Thesis Guideline, 4th Edition, Section B1** for Agriculture, Science, and Technology disciplines, alongside international postgraduate science benchmarks).

---

## 1. Strict Chapter Arrangement (Section B1)

Every science-based thesis must follow this unbending chapter structure:

```
1.0 Introduction
    1.1 Background of the Study
    1.2 Statement of the Problem & Justification
    1.3 Aim and Objectives
    1.4 Research Questions / Hypotheses
    1.5 Significance & Scope of the Study
2.0 Literature Review
    2.1 Conceptual / Theoretical Framework
    2.2 Thematic Review of Prior Empirical Findings
    2.3 Methodological Review & Contradiction Analysis
    2.4 Summary of Gaps Addressed by the Study
3.0 Materials and Methods
    3.1 Study Location & Environmental Conditions (Maps & GPS Coordinates)
    3.2 Materials, Reagents & Equipment
    3.3 Experimental Design & Sampling Protocols
    3.4 Analytical & Experimental Procedures
    3.5 Statistical Modeling & Software Implementations
4.0 Results
    4.1 Prose-First Narrative Presentation of Observations
    4.2 Sequentially Arranged Visuals (Separate-Page Placement)
5.0 Discussion
    5.1 Synthesis of Findings in Relation to Published Literature
    5.2 Inductive & Deductive Evaluation of Initial Hypotheses
    5.3 Mechanistic Explanations & Disciplinary Implications
    5.4 Binomial Nomenclature & Study Limitations
6.0 Conclusion and Recommendations
    6.1 Conclusion
    6.2 Recommendation(s)
    6.3 Contribution to Knowledge / Developmental Challenge Addressed
References (Harvard Style: Name-and-Year System)
Appendices (Ph.D. Mandatory Journal Publication, Raw Data, Instruments, Code)
```

## 2. Chapter-by-Chapter Technical Requirements

### Chapter 1.0: Introduction
* **Purpose:** Establish the empirical backdrop, societal/scientific subject, explicit purpose, and operational scope of the investigation.
* **Core Components:**
  * **Background:** High-density scholarly context outlining the evolution and current status of the problem.
  * **Justification & Problem Statement:** Articulate the precise empirical bottleneck, theoretical breakdown, or practical failure motivating the inquiry.
  * **Aim and Objectives:** State the overarching broad aim, followed immediately by numbered specific operational objectives ($1, 2, 3 \dots$) designed to achieve the aim.
  * **Scope:** Explicit geographical, temporal, taxonomic, and methodological boundaries.
* **Strict Length Cap:** Must not exceed **three (3) pages** in standard formatted double-spaced text. Keep prose dense, focused, and free of introductory fluff.

### Chapter 2.0: Literature Review
* **Content:** Synthesize professional knowledge, empirical discoveries, and competing paradigms from previous researchers to illuminate the problem under study. Must incorporate conflicting opinions, methodological divergences, and competing hypotheses.
* **Recency Requirement:** At least **70% of all cited works must be published within the last ten (10) years** (e.g., 2016–2026 for a 2026 submission), except for foundational classical theories or standard historical methodologies.
* **Citation Synchronization:** 100% bidirectional correspondence: every single inline citation must appear in the References list, and every reference listed must be cited inline in the text.
* **Synthesis Standard:** Employ the 3:1 multi-source synthesis rule. Avoid serial paper-by-paper summaries ("Author A did X... Author B did Y..."). Organize by conceptual themes directly addressing the study's specific objectives. Maintain plagiarism similarity strictly below 4%.

### Chapter 3.0: Materials and Methods
* **Level of Detail:** Detail materials, biological reagents, equipment models, environmental conditions, precautions, calibration steps, and analytical protocols with sufficient precision to enable complete independent verification and replication by other scientists.
* **Study Location & Coordinates:** You must explicitly identify the exact place/location of the research. For field sampling, farm trials, or ecological/environmental surveys, **maps and GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, and elevation) are mandatory**.
* **Experimental Design:** Explicitly specify the experimental layout or design (e.g., Randomized Complete Block Design [RCBD], Completely Randomized Design [CRD], Split-Plot Design, Multi-stage Stratified Clustered Sampling), treatment levels, replication factors, and blocking criteria.
* **Statistical Analysis:** State the formal mathematical and statistical models, null/alternative hypotheses, significance thresholds ($\alpha = 0.05, 0.01$), and exact software packages used (including software version numbers, packages/libraries, and specific algorithm configurations).

### Chapter 4.0: Results
* **Prose-First Presentation Rule:** Data and observations must be presented in prose (text) form first. The prose must narrate the quantitative trends, percentage changes, statistical significance, and observed variances before referring the reader to any graphic.
* **Placement of Visuals:** Tables, Figures, and Plates must be arranged sequentially on **separate pages** immediately after the prose explanations for that section. Never embed tables mid-paragraph.
* **Anti-Duplication Standard:** Do not duplicate identical values across text, tables, and figures. The text synthesizes salient findings and critical contrasts; the tables/figures provide comprehensive data records.
* **Captions & Numbering:**
  * **Tables:** Numbered sequentially by chapter (e.g., Table 4.1, Table 4.2); title placed at the **top** of the table.
  * **Figures & Plates:** Numbered sequentially by chapter (e.g., Figure 4.1, Plate 4.1); title placed at the **bottom** of the figure/plate.
  * **Typography:** Bulky tables may reduce font size to 10 pt Times New Roman with single line spacing to maintain structural clarity.

### Chapter 5.0: Discussion
* **Analysis & Synthesis:** Review and analyze the experimental or empirical results in direct relation to published scientific literature and the study's initial hypotheses.
* **Argumentation Logic:** Deploy rigorous inductive and deductive arguments to highlight both the strengths and weaknesses of the findings.
* **Mechanistic Depth:** Explain *why* observed phenomena occurred (e.g., physiological, biochemical, statistical, or agronomic mechanisms), rather than merely restating the numbers.
* **Contradiction Resolution:** Contrast results against previous works cited in Chapter 2.0. Explicitly explain points of agreement, novel departures, or apparent contradictions using the two-stage contradiction resolution framework (theoretical conflict vs. scope variation).
* **Taxonomic / Scientific Nomenclature:** Strictly follow the international binomial system of nomenclature for all biological organisms (e.g., *Meloidogyne incognita*, *Zea mays* L.). The generic name must be capitalized and the species name in lowercase; both must be italicized. State the full binomial name with taxonomic authority at first mention, abbreviating the genus subsequently (*M. incognita*).
* **Boundary Conditions:** Acknowledge genuine study limitations, experimental constraints, and anomalous outliers objectively.

### Chapter 6.0: Conclusion and Recommendations
This chapter must be strictly partitioned into three distinct, numbered sub-sections:
* **6.1 Conclusion:**
  * Synthesizes the primary inferences drawn from factual evidence established in the study.
  * Must be systematically structured and guided by the specific objectives stated in Chapter 1.0.
  * No speculation or new data may be introduced in the conclusion.
* **6.2 Recommendation(s):**
  * Practical, policy, agronomic, industrial, or technological suggestions.
  * Must be derived **strictly from the results** of the study. Speculative or generic recommendations ("more research should be done") are prohibited.
* **6.3 Contribution to Knowledge / Developmental Challenge Addressed:**
  * Itemized declaration of the original scientific, theoretical, or methodological additions to the discipline.
  * Explicit explanation of the local, national, or international developmental challenges solved or addressed by the findings.

## 3. Formatting & Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Notes / Enforcement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paper Standard** | A4 size (210 mm $\times$ 297 mm), 80 gsm | Typed on one side of the paper only. |
| **Binding Margin (Left)** | **4.0 cm** (1.57 inches) | Allows sufficient gutter margin for hardbound volume sewing. |
| **Other Margins (Right, Top, Bottom)**| **2.5 cm** (1.0 inch) | Uniform margins across all pages. |
| **Typeface / Font** | Times New Roman | Size 12 or 14 for body text (depending on bulkiness). Size 10 permitted for bulky tables. |
| **Line Spacing** | Double line spacing | Mandatory for all body text. Single spacing reserved for Abstract, References, long Tables, and indented block quotes. |
| **Pagination: Preliminary Pages** | Lowercase Roman numerals (`i, ii, iii \dots`) | Centralized at the **bottom** of the page. Title page counts as page `i` but does not show a number. |
| **Pagination: Main Text** | Arabic numerals (`1, 2, 3 \dots`) | Centralized at the **top** of the page, beginning from Introduction (1.0) through Appendices. |
| **Abstract Format** | Informative style, standalone | Single paragraph, single line spacing, strictly maximum **500 words**. Must cover: objectives, methodology, quantitative key findings, conclusion, recommendations. |
| **Referencing Style** | Harvard Style (Name and Year system) | Alphabetical order by author surname. Format: Author, A. B. (Year). Title. Journal, Vol(Issue): Pages. |
| **Citation Recency** | $\ge 70\%$ within last 10 years | Mandatory check for external defense readiness. |
| **Ph.D. Publication Mandate** | At least one (1) published paper | Must include evidence of publication in an international, peer-reviewed, high-impact journal attached as an Appendix. |

## 4. Visual Arrangement & Formatting Protocol

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PROSE NARRATIVE (Main Body Page)                                       │
│ "Evaluation of treatment effects revealed a statistically significant   │
│ 34.2% reduction in root galling index relative to the untreated        │
│ control (p < 0.001), as detailed in Table 4.3..."                      │
└────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┘
                                     │
                                     ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ SEPARATE PAGE (Immediately following prose narrative)                  │
│                                                                        │
│ Table 4.3: Influence of Botanical Amendments on Nematode Galling       │
│ ┌──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐ │
│ │ Treatment            │ Galling Index (0-5)  │ Juvenile Count / 100g│ │
│ ├──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┤ │
│ │ Control              │ 4.82 ± 0.12          │ 1,420 ± 45           │ │
│ │ Azadirachta indica   │ 1.64 ± 0.08          │ 210 ± 18             │ │
│ └──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘ │
│ Values represent mean ± standard error (N = 5).                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Table Rules:** Top caption, no vertical grid lines, clean horizontal rules (booktabs style), footnotes immediately below table body.
* **Figure & Plate Rules:** Bottom caption, high-resolution graphics, scale bars and legends clearly labeled.
* **Plates:** Photographs of experimental setups, field sites, plant pathology symptoms, or anatomical sections must be titled "Plate X.X".

## 5. Thesis Examiner Pre-Defense Audit Rubric

When auditing or reviewing thesis chapters, test against these ten examiner rejection gates:

1. **Chapter 1.0 Page Count:** Does the introduction exceed 3 pages? (Immediate rejection if > 3 pages).
2. **Objective-Alignment:** Does every operational objective in 1.3 map 1-to-1 to a results sub-section in 4.0, a discussion theme in 5.0, and a conclusion in 6.1?
3. **GPS Grounding:** If field sampling or agricultural trials are conducted, are exact GPS coordinates and location maps provided in 3.1?
4. **Statistical Package Precision:** Are software names, exact version numbers, and computational libraries declared in 3.5?
5. **Prose-First Results Check:** Does the text discuss the data thoroughly before presenting the table or figure callout?
6. **Visual Separation Check:** Are tables and figures placed on dedicated separate pages immediately following prose rather than embedded mid-text?
7. **Scientific Binomial Formatting:** Are biological species written in valid binomial nomenclature (*Genus species*), italicized, with genus capitalized and species lowercase?
8. **Section 6 Partitioning:** Is Chapter 6 strictly divided into 6.1 (Conclusion), 6.2 (Recommendation(s)), and 6.3 (Contribution to Knowledge / Developmental Challenges)?
9. **Reference Recency ($\ge 70\%$ in last 10 yrs):** Do at least 70% of citations fall within the last decade?
10. **Abstract Word Cap:** Is the abstract a single paragraph, single-spaced, under 500 words, containing quantitative key findings?
